Steps to Prepare for NPTEL

Step-by-Step Preparation

NPTEL (National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning) offers online courses from IITs and IISc.

  • Choose the right course based on your syllabus
  • Watch weekly video lectures
  • Download and revise lecture notes
  • Attempt weekly assignments
  • Revise before the final exam

Preparation Tips

  • Make short notes
  • Follow a weekly timetable
  • Refer to previous year questions
  • Clear doubts using discussion forum

Consistency is the key factor to succeed in NPTEL courses.
